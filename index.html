About the Project

I built this project out of an interest in supply chains, logistics, international trade, technology and data analysis. What started as an investigation of U.S. exports developed into a broader attempt to understand how trade statistics relate to domestic production and the movement of goods through the economy.

A major part of the work has involved reconciling datasets and definitions, checking assumptions, revising calculations when stronger information became available, and distinguishing official observations from modeled estimates. The project is continuing to evolve as additional data is verified and the analytical framework is refined.

Data Analysis Organizing large trade and economic datasets into comparable industries, products and geographic categories.
Audit & Validation Reviewing definitions, identifying inconsistencies and separating observed statistics from estimates and provisional values.
Interactive Research Turning the analysis into browser-based tools that make economic relationships easier to explore and understand.
